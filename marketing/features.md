## 2) Choose your go‑to‑market model

You can run **three parallel models**:

### A) Direct sales (your own team / you)

- You close deals directly with end clients (SMEs).  
- Higher margin, more control, slower scale.

### B) Reseller / agency partners

- Local IT companies, business setup consultants, travel agencies, accounting firms become your **sales agents**.  
- They sell your ERP to their clients; you give them **commission or revenue share**.  
- Fast scale, lower margin per deal.

### C) Freelance “software hunters” (commission‑only salespeople)

- Freelancers with SME networks (ex‑bankers, ex‑telco, ex‑POS vendors).  
- Paid per closed deal (fixed SAR or % of first‑year revenue).  
- Good for city‑by‑city coverage (Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam).

Your “agency/freelancer hunting” plan focuses on **B + C**.

### Step 5 – Design the partner/freelancer program

Make it simple and attractive:

- **Commission structure** (example):  
  - 25% of first‑year license fee per client.  
  - 10% recurring while client is active.  
  - Bonus for hitting targets (e.g., extra 5% after 10 clients/year).

- **Support you provide**:  
  - Demo access, sales scripts, email/WhatsApp templates.  
  - 1–2 hour training session for their team.  
  - Joint demo calls with their end clients when needed.

- **Rules**:  
  - Deal registration (they register client name/email to avoid conflicts).  
  - Payment terms (you invoice client; you pay commission monthly/quarterly).  
  - Non‑compete clause if needed (they don’t push direct competitors while promoting you).

Document this in a **Partner Agreement** (even a simple 2–3 page PDF).
