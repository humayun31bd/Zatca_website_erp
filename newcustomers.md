New customers: first-time buyers, often influenced by promotions or word of mouth
Repeat customers: have bought before and return periodically
Loyal customers: buy consistently, often advocate for the brand, and are least price-sensitive
Impulse buyers: purchase spontaneously with little research
Need-based customers: buy only when they have a specific need
Discount-driven customers: respond mainly to sales and deals
Lapsed customers: used to buy but have stopped
